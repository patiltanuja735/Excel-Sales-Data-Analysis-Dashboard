# 📊 Sales Data Analysis Dashboard – Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data using Microsoft Excel and creating an interactive Sales Dashboard to track business performance and identify meaningful sales trends.

The dashboard provides a visual overview of sales performance across different regions, product categories, customer segments, shipping methods, and months. It allows users to explore the data using interactive slicers and understand important business metrics.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and business trends.
- Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare sales across different regions and cities.
- Understand monthly sales patterns.
- Analyze product categories and customer segments.
- Evaluate shipping methods and their contribution.
- Provide an interactive dashboard for better business decision-making.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes four major KPIs:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 5,820,182.28 |
| Average Sales | 25,123.00 |
| Total Transactions | 9,994 |
| Total Quantity | 582.37 |

### 2. Sales Analysis and Visualizations

- **Shipping Mode Analysis:** Pie chart showing the distribution of First Class, Same Day, Second Class, and Standard Class.
- **Regional Sales Analysis:** Donut chart comparing sales performance across Central, East, South, and West regions.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Line chart displaying sales performance from January to December.
- **City-wise Sales Analysis:** Column chart highlighting sales across major cities, including New York City, Los Angeles, Philadelphia, Seattle, and San Francisco.
- **Category Analysis:** Horizontal bar chart comparing Technology, Office Supplies, and Furniture.
- **Customer Segment Analysis:** Bar chart showing sales distribution across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard

- Region slicer to filter data by Central, East, South, and West.
- Year slicer to analyze yearly sales performance.
- Interactive charts that allow users to explore different business dimensions.
